# Action audit

How Spool records AUDIT-001's facts. The contract clause states what the audit must show; this page describes how the writers commit it. It was moved from the AUDIT-001 Status row of CONTRACT.md in the 28 October 2026 contract cleanup.

Every Spool writer commits its change and one attributed safe fact in the same D1 transaction: owner, deploy, ingest and run-token batches (and the scheduled deployment-deadline service) open a context holding the actor, action, subject and project aliases, and the operational journal row or an explicit commit inside that batch records the fact, so a denied, stale or aborted batch records none; a journal row written without a context is refused by the schema. Denied, stale and failed attempts keep request observations with truthful outcomes; sign-in and Business import doors are attributed to the owner or service they admitted. Erasure completion commits a service fact in its final erasure batch; webhook and mail notification attempts and private archive and audit exports record `started` before the effect and exactly one outcome after it. Journal rows written before attribution have value-free legacy facts (kind-derived action, original time, unknown actor, fresh unlinkable labels); retention writes its journal row once instead of revising it. Private archives carry the safe chronology as a payload and never the private alias links; restore appends it, with original ids, times, sources, aliases and outcomes, after the destination's own facts in the restore's commit, then records the restore; previous archive formats gain legacy facts. Owner-only fixed-snapshot cursor history and JSON/CSV export; restart; actor/project erasure deletes private links and leaves prior facts unchanged. Base-owned operator-auth and import-kit writes (invitations, roles, Sentry/Business import plan/apply/undo) are attributed by their request observation, not inside the Base transaction.
